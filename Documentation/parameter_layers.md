# Parameter Layers

- **Pilot capability parameters** influence how the pilot chooses actions or interprets context.
- **Mecha capability parameters** influence what the suit can do and execution reliability.
- **Environment/task parameters** influence the task universe itself.
- **Analysis-only fields** are logging/metric fields not used for pilot/mecha/environment action.

`teamwork_potential` and `taskwork_potential` are high-level manipulation variables whose downstream mechanisms may span layers.
